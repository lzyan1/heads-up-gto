Study settings

Full tree solves the flop, turn and river together with no neural networks, so every street can mix; river and turn solves finish in seconds, a flop can take minutes to an hour and needs up to about 9 GiB of memory. It reports exploitability and is an approximate equilibrium of the fixed betting tree. Legacy uses one action per hand on the flop and turn and calls a direct river solver; it is not a full-game GTO certificate.
